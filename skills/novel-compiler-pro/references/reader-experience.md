# Reader Experience

Use this file to adapt oh-story-style reader focus into the compiler.

## Reader Promise

Every project must define `memory/reader_promise.md`:

- What emotion does the reader come for?
- What curiosity keeps them reading?
- What relationship, power, justice, mystery, comfort, or awe promise is active?
- What platform expectation matters, if any?

Reader promise must influence chapter planning and chapter gates.

## Chapter Reader Check

For each chapter, check:

1. Entry image is concrete.
2. The reader quickly knows what is at stake.
3. The chapter has a hook, pressure, or question.
4. Emotional target is clear.
5. The chapter changes story state.
6. There is a payoff, escalation, reversal, or sharper question.
7. Ending leaves momentum.

## Hook Types

Use hooks deliberately:

- `疑问钩子`: a concrete unanswered question.
- `危机钩子`: immediate danger or deadline.
- `关系钩子`: a changed relationship state.
- `信息钩子`: a reveal that reframes prior events.
- `代价钩子`: success creates a new cost.
- `误判钩子`: a character's assumption is likely wrong.
- `承诺钩子`: a promised scene readers want to see.

Do not use vague hooks like “一切才刚刚开始” unless the scene has earned a specific reason.

## Emotional Payoff

Each chapter should know its emotional job:

- curiosity
- relief
- dread
- awe
- tenderness
- comedy
- righteous satisfaction
- humiliation/reversal
- grief
- anticipation
- pressure

Emotion should be delivered through scene action, dialogue, choice, and consequence, not author explanation.

## Commercial Web-Novel Checks

Use only when the project targets web-novel platforms.

Check:

- clear chapter hook
- readable scene progression
- low-friction opening
- regular expectation/reward cycle
- protagonist agency
- emotional promise is not forgotten
- important payoff is delayed but not endlessly stalled
- chapter ending encourages next-click behavior

Avoid forcing web-novel tactics onto literary, experimental, or quiet realist projects unless requested.

## Deslop Pass

Run before final or when prose feels generic.

Remove or revise:

- meta narration
- TODO or bracketed notes
- essay-like explanation
- dialogue that only dumps setting
- repeated triadic sentence patterns
- vague abstractions replacing concrete action
- generic AI phrases such as “某种意义上”, “仿佛整个世界”, “他终于意识到” when unsupported
- over-neat emotional summaries
- excessive rhetorical questions
- identical paragraph rhythm

Preserve:

- plot events
- character facts
- foreshadowing
- voice traits
- deliberate style choices
